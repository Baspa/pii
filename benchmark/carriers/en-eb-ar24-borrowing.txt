The ECB is responsible for the administration of accounts and processing of payments relating to the borrowing and lending operations of the EU under the medium-term financial assistance (MTFA) facility for Member States’ balances of payments, the European Financial Stabilisation Mechanism (EFSM), the European instrument for temporary Support to mitigate Unemployment Risks in an Emergency (SURE), the Next Generation EU (NGEU) programme, the Ukraine Facility, including exceptional bridge financing, and short-term macro-financial assistance to the Arab Republic of Egypt. The ECB is also responsible for the administration of certain payments arising in connection with operations under the European Financial Stability Facility (EFSF) and the European Stability Mechanism (ESM), and for processing all payments in relation to the loan facility agreement for Greece. In 2024 the ECB performed paying agent services for the European Commission.

As at 31 December 2024 the total outstanding nominal amount was €0.2 billion under the MTFA facility, €42 billion under the EFSM, €98.35 billion under SURE and €31.605 billion under the loan facility agreement for Greece. Finally, in 2024 the ECB processed disbursements and interest payments of NGEU loans and grants to and from various Member States, also including loans to Ukraine.
